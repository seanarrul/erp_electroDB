title: "Bug Report: Sales Management Pagination Resets to Page 1"

##Bug Report:## Sales Table Pagination Next Button (`>`) Resets to Page 1


### Summary
On the **Sales Management** table, clicking the Next pagination button ( > ) to view records 11–20 (Page 2 of 13) fails to update the table view. Instead of advancing to Page 2, the control immediately resets/reverts back to Page 1 (1 / 13).


### Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the **Sales Management** dashboard.
2. Locate the pagination controls at the bottom right of the sales records table (showing `Showing 1-10 of 124` and page count `1 / 13`).
3. Click the right arrow / Next page button (`>`).


### Expected Result
- The pagination indicator updates from `1 / 13` to `2 / 13`.
- The table status updates to `Showing 11-20 of 124`.
- The table list refreshes to display transactions starting from `TR000114` downwards.


This pagination bug is isolated to the **Sales Management** page. The Next button (`>`) was tested and works fine across all other entity modules, including **Customer**, **Payments**, **Products**, and **Human Resources**.
