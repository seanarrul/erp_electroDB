# Test Suite: Per-Right Permission Matrix for Customer and Product 


Permission Right: Customer Add
- Role with Customer Add Right: Add Customer button visible and enabled
- Role without Customer Add Right: Add Customer button hidden or disabled

Permission Right: Customer Edit
- Role with Customer Edit Right: Edit button visible and enabled
- Role without Customer Edit Right: Edit button hidden or disabled

Permission Right: Customer Delete / Restore
- Role with Customer Delete Right: Delete and Restore controls visible and enabled
* Role without Customer Delete Right: Delete and Restore controls hidden or disabled

Permission Right: Product Add
- Role with Product Add Right: Add Product button visible and enabled
- Role without Product Add Right: Add Product button hidden or disabled

Permission Right: Product Edit 
- Role with Product Edit Right: Edit button visible and general fields editable
- Role without Product Edit Right: Edit button hidden or disabled

Permission Right: Product Delete / Restore
- Role with Product Delete Right: Delete and Restore controls visible and enabled; DELETE 
- Role without Product Delete Right: Delete and Restore controls hidden or disabled; direct DELETE 

Permission Right: Product Price Rights (View & Edit Price)
- Role with Price Rights: Price values and Price History visible; price input field editable during add/edit; server accepts price updates
- Role without Price Rights: Price editing field is read-only or hidden
