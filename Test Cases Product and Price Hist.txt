# Test Cases: Product and Price History 


PROD-01: Add New Product with Initial Price
Feature: Create Product
Pre-conditions: User is logged in with product add and price rights
Test Steps:
1. Navigate to the Products module and click Add Product
2. Enter product details (Product Name, Category, Initial Price)
3. Click Save
Expected Result: Product is created with the specified initial price, and an initial entry is logged in its price history.

PROD-02: Edit Product Details 
Feature: Update Product
Pre-conditions: An active product exists
Test Steps:
1. Open an existing product for editing
2. Update the product name or description without changing the price
3. Click Save
Expected Result: Product details update successfully, and no duplicate price history entry is created.

PROD-03: Update Product Price and Record History
Feature: Price History Logging
Pre-conditions: An active product exists with an initial price
Test Steps:
1. Open the product and update its price to a new value
2. Save the changes
3. Open the product Price History view
Expected Result: The current price updates to the new value, and the Price History log displays the previous price, new price, date/time of change, and the user who made the update.

PROD-04: Price History Chronological Order
Feature: Price History Audit Trail
Pre-conditions: A product has had multiple price updates
Test Steps:
1. View the Price History section of the product
Expected Result: All historical price changes are listed in clear chronological order without overwriting past entries.

PROD-05: Invalid Price Input Validation
Feature: Price Validation
Pre-conditions: Add or Edit Product form is open
Test Steps:
1. Enter a negative number or non-numeric characters in the price field
2. Click Save
Expected Result: System blocks saving and displays an error indicating the price must be a valid positive number.
